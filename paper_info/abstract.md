# Abstract

This project presents a lightweight decision-centric Geo-AI framework for energy-aware environmental monitoring under spatial and operational constraints.

The framework models environmental monitoring as a simulated spatial decision-making problem involving:
- dynamic environmental risk,
- terrain-dependent energy consumption,
- monitoring coverage dynamics,
- partial observability.

An autonomous monitoring agent operates using:
- rule-based feasibility constraints,
- explicit utility-based reasoning,
- lightweight online outcome learning.

Unlike end-to-end policy learning approaches, the framework emphasizes:
- interpretability,
- transparent decision-making,
- computational simplicity,
- explicit trade-offs between monitoring objectives.

The experimental evaluation compares:
- constraint-only agents,
- utility-based agents,
- utility + outcome learning agents.

The experiments analyze:
- energy consumption,
- monitoring coverage,
- sustainability,
- robustness under environmental volatility.

Results demonstrate that lightweight utility reasoning combined with lightweight outcome learning can support effective and interpretable environmental monitoring behavior without requiring computationally expensive black-box policy learning.
